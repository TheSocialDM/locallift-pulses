Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what LocalLift Signature, a service of The SocialDM ("we", "us"), collects through localliftsignature.com and our emails, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, email, phone, brokerage, website, tagline, social profile links, ZIP codes or city, brand color, the call to action you choose, and the headshot and logo you upload.
  • Payment details. Payments are processed by PayPal. We receive your subscription reference and payment status. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Site usage. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to tell us how visitors find and use the site, such as pages viewed and whether a form was completed.
  • Email activity. Our emails may record whether a message was delivered and opened, so we can fix delivery problems.

How we use it

  • To create and deliver your posts, free reports and newsletters.
  • To send service emails such as your welcome email, weekly deliveries and billing notices.
  • To send The Signature Brief and related tips if you signed up for them. Every one includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve the site and the service, and to protect against fraud and misuse.

Your headshot, logo, name and contact details appear on the posts we make for you, because that is the service.

Who we share it with

We share information only with companies that help us run the service, such as website hosting, form handling, email delivery, payment processing, analytics, the AI and design tools that help us create your posts and reports, and, if you connect accounts, the scheduling tools that publish your posts. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you subscribe or stay on our lists, and for a reasonable time after to meet legal, tax and record keeping needs. Ask us and we will delete what we no longer need to keep.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in any email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing info@thesocialdm.com.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us right away if you think something is wrong.

Children

Signature is for working professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We will post any update here with a new effective date, and email subscribers about material changes. Questions: info@thesocialdm.com.
