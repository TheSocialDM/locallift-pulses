Questions, answered.

Do I have to approve every post?

No. You approve your headshot, logo and details once when you sign up. After that, your posts arrive every week, ready to share, and publish on their own once your business pages are connected. Want to stop? Cancel anytime through PayPal or email us.

Is there a free trial?

Start with the free AI report. It shows how AI sees you today, at no cost. Signature itself is month to month with no contract, so you can cancel anytime.

Will another agent get the same posts?

No. Your posts are built for your ZIP codes and your brand, and carry your name. Agents who share a ZIP code may see the same market numbers, written and designed differently.

Where do the market numbers come from?

From trusted local market data for your area, checked before every post. The data date is printed on every post that uses numbers.

Do I have to film anything?

No. Every post is made for you from your photo, your logo and local data.

Can you guarantee I’ll show up in ChatGPT or Google?

No one can honestly promise that. We create fresh, specific, local posts under your name every week, which is what search engines and AI assistants look for.
