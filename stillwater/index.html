Where to put the ad money next

Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 vs Aug 10 to Sep 6
GA4 · our read

Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: hold the budget and move it toward the campaigns that produce leadsSpend was steady at $4,498 against $4,538, and website leads stayed level (24 to 23), so each lead cost $196 in these four weeks against $189 before. Performance Max produced 14 leads at $85 each, while In Location spent $1,859 with no website leads. Ask the vendor whether In Location brings calls or form leads of its own, and if not, shift part of it toward Performance Max and recheck after two weeks. Also check bidding on your own name: 66% of search clicks already knew "Stillwater Lakes", and the listing and plain results catch many of them for free. A short test pausing name ads shows whether they add leases.

Hold

Other paid sources: ask Facebook, Instagram and geofencing for proof before renewing1,356 visits and no website leads in four weeks. These ads can collect leads in their own forms, so ask the vendor for those leads and any leases tied to them. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins. Clicks often get cheaper in winter when rivals pull back.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhen occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many units come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Scale

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 689.268.1980 while the website shows 689.314.6918, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. Answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 689.268.1980.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 1,125 of 1,741 organic visits (65%) and 29 of 41 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

It sent 1,865 visits and 23 website leads, one lead per 81 visits, and it is the second largest lead source. Spend was $4,498 in these four weeks against $4,538 before, so each website lead cost $196 against $189. Performance Max produced 14 leads at $85 each; In Location spent $1,859 with no website leads. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask the vendor for those before judging any one campaign.

Are the Facebook, Instagram and geofencing ads working?

On the website, together they sent 1,356 visits and no leads, and most visitors left without engaging. These ads can collect leads in their own forms, so ask each vendor for its lead count for the same four weeks before judging the spend.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read and change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (100) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (94 visits) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How many searchers find us without our name?

378 search clicks came from searches like "apartments near me", up 10% on the four weeks before. That is one in three; the rest came from searches for Stillwater.
