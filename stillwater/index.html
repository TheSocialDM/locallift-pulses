Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: hold the budget and move it toward the campaigns that produce leadsSpend fell to $4,349 from $4,510 while website leads went from 23 to 15, so each lead cost $290 in these four weeks against $196 before. Performance Max produced 6 leads at $191 each, while In Location spent $1,745 for 2 website leads. Ask the vendor whether In Location brings calls or form leads of its own, and if not, shift part of it toward Performance Max and recheck after two weeks. Also check bidding on your own name: 71% of search clicks (Oct 14 to Nov 10) already knew "Stillwater Lakes", and the listing and plain results catch many of them for free. A short test pausing name ads shows whether they add leases.

Hold

Other paid sources: ask Facebook, Instagram and geofencing for proof before renewing333 visits and no website leads in four weeks. These ads can collect leads in their own forms, so ask the vendor for those leads and any leases tied to them. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins. Clicks often get cheaper in winter when rivals pull back.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhen occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many units come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records

What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 689.268.1980 while the website shows 689.314.6918, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. Answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 689.268.1980.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 672 of 1,129 organic visits (60%) and 8 of 14 organic leads (Oct 16 to Nov 12) started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

From Oct 16 to Nov 12 it sent 1,195 visits and 15 website leads, one lead per 80 visits, against the 13 from your listing and plain search results together. Spend was $4,349 in these four weeks against $4,510 before, so each website lead cost $290 against $196. Performance Max produced 6 leads at $191 each; In Location spent $1,745 for 2 website leads. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask the vendor for those before judging any one campaign.

Are the Facebook, Instagram and geofencing ads working?

On the website, together they sent 333 visits and no leads from Oct 16 to Nov 12, and most visitors left without engaging. These ads can collect leads in their own forms, so ask each vendor for its lead count for the same four weeks before judging the spend.

Why do some cards end on different dates?

Each source publishes on its own schedule, and every card uses its own source's newest complete day: the website to November 12, search to November 10 and the Google listing to November 9, because Google publishes listing figures about five days late. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (94, Oct 13 to Nov 9; Google publishes listing figures about five days late) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (53 visits, Oct 16 to Nov 12) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How many searchers find us without our name?

242 search clicks (Oct 14 to Nov 10) came from searches like "apartments near me", down 29% on the 28 days before. That is about three in ten; the rest came from searches for Stillwater.
