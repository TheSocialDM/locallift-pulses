Where to put the ad money next

Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 vs Aug 10 to Sep 6
GA4 · our read

Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: keep it running while Montage leases up, and fix what raised its cost per leadSpend rose 16% ($3,726 to $4,327) in these four weeks while website leads fell from 19 to 9, so each website lead cost $481, up from $196. Three campaigns (Outside Location, In Location Campaign, Branded In Location) took $2,398 with no website leads, while Demand Gen brought 6 at $133 each. They may be earning calls or Google form leads this page cannot see, so ask the vendor for those first; if there are none, shift budget toward what produces leases. With homes still open, fix the mix before cutting the budget.

Fix

Other paid sources: ask Facebook ads and Zillow for proof before renewingFacebook ads sent 1,277 visits and no website leads, after 1,244 visits and none the four weeks before; Zillow sent 155 visits and none. Both can collect leads on their own sites, so ask each for its lead count and any leases tied to them for September 7 to October 4. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopIn the Portland area, as in most markets, leasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring. For a lease up, trim only the weakest terms in winter, keep your name and high intent searches such as "apartments tigard" running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhile homes are still open, spend should generally keep pace with them as long as cost per lease stays below what a lease is worth. Add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits, and step paid search on general terms down as occupancy nears its target.

Rule

How to scale a winnerOnce a paid channel shows a cost per lease you are happy with, raise it about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 971.287.5814 while the website shows 971.399.3242, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. If it does, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 971.287.5814.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 654 of 1,094 organic visits (60%) and 6 of 11 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working, and what moved leads?

It sent 1,447 visits and 9 website leads, half of the 18 Google leads, on $4,327 of spend: $481 a website lead, against $196 in the four weeks before ($3,726 for 19 leads). Website leads fell from 44 to 32, and 10 of the 12 lost leads were Google Ads leads, so most of the drop sits there. Demand Gen produced 6 of the campaigns' 8 leads; three campaigns spent $2,398 with none. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask the vendor for those before judging. See Google Ads by campaign.

Are the Facebook ads working?

On the website, they sent 1,277 visits and no website leads from September 7 to October 4, and only 26% of those visits engaged. If the ads use Facebook lead forms, those leads stay inside Facebook; check the ad account before deciding on the budget.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read and change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (50) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (21 visits) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How is search trending?

Average position improved by 3 places and a larger share of searchers clicked. Google showed the site 26% less often, so the dip in clicks came from fewer appearances, not weaker results. Clicks on searches containing "montage" fell 22%, from 447 to 347.
