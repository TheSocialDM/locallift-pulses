Strategic read

Opportunity & brand risk

The opportunity

Own “lakefront” and “new construction” in Kissimmee.

These are the questions where AI already picks Stillwater. Perplexity named it first for lakefront apartments and for new construction now leasing; ChatGPT named it second for lakefront, for the 34744 zip code and for Partin Settlement Road and US 192; Gemini named it second near Lake Toho and downtown Kissimmee. On broader questions such as the best apartments in Kissimmee, apartments near Lake Nona, or townhomes for rent, Stillwater did not come up in any of the three engines. Content built on the lakefront and new construction story, tied to those places, is how that reach widens.

The brand risk

Stillwater is a shared name.

In September, Google showed the site for searches about other places with similar names, such as “still water lake estates” (96 appearances), “stillwater apartments slc”, “stillwater palms” and “the cottages at stillwater”. Those renters were looking for somewhere else. Using the full name “Stillwater Lakes, Kissimmee FL” the same way on every listing, post and page helps Google and AI keep this community separate from the others.
