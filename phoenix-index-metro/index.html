        06

        Method and definitions

        How a production edition of the index would be built. In this sample, every number is generated for illustration.

        Renter prompts
        Each month, a fixed library of plain language renter questions is asked about every submarket, for example "best apartments in Tempe near the light rail" or "pet friendly apartments in Gilbert under $2,000". Prompts are repeated across four major AI assistants in fresh sessions with no prior history.
        Recommendation rate
        The share of answers to a submarket's prompts that name the community as a place to consider. Rates within a submarket can add up to more than 100% because one answer usually names several communities.
        Assistant coverage
        How many of the four assistants name the community at least once in the month. Shown as four dots.
        Average position
        Where the community appears in the answer when it is named. 1.0 means it is always listed first.
        AI Apartment Index score (0 to 100)
        A weighted blend of recommendation rate, assistant coverage and average position, scaled so scores compare across submarkets of different sizes.
        Leader agreement
        How many of the four assistants put the submarket leader first more often than any other community.
