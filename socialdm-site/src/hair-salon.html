Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with independent stylists and suite owners?

Yes. Independent stylists and suite owners get a simple monthly plan built around their chair. Salon owners and groups get a plan scoped to the team and locations.

Can you post my real work?

Yes, and it performs best. Share photos and videos of your work and we turn them into polished posts. Guests only appear with their permission.

Do I need to be on every platform?

No. We focus on where your guests actually look, usually Instagram, TikTok and your Google profile, and keep them consistent.

Can you help with Google reviews?

Yes. We help you ask happy guests for reviews and answer every review in your voice.

Do you run paid ads?

When they fit the plan. Most salons get more from a strong Google profile, steady reviews and fresh work online before spending on ads.
