FAQ

Questions we hear

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. You own your domain and your content. We build and keep the pages current as part of your plan.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Usually not. Most businesses get more from one to three focused landing pages added to the site they already have, tied to their Google profile, posts and reviews.

How long until the pages show up on Google?

We ask Google to index new pages as soon as they go live. How fast Google adds them to search varies, often a few days to a few weeks, and newer domains take longer.

Is the website a separate project?

No. Sites and landing pages are part of your visibility plan, so the pages, your Google profile and your social all work from the same plan.
