Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. The SocialDM, LLC, Arizona.

This policy explains what The SocialDM, LLC (“The SocialDM,” “we” or “us”) collects through thesocialdm.com and our other sites that link to this policy, including moveinlocal.com and themultifamilydesk.com, and how we use it. LocalLift Signature has its own policy at localliftsignature.com/privacy.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you use our contact form or request a report, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone, company, city, what you are interested in and your message.
  • Site usage. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure visits, pages viewed and how people find the site.
  • Email activity. When we email you, our email providers record delivery, opens and clicks so we can fix delivery problems and improve our emails.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and send what you asked for, including a copy of your message and any report you requested.
  • To follow up about our services when you have contacted us. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • If you share a phone number, we use it only to reply to you. We do not send marketing texts.
  • To understand how the site is used, improve it and protect it from spam and misuse.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the site and our business, such as website hosting, form handling, email delivery, workflow automation and analytics. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies’ advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we are in contact with you or working together, plus a reasonable period for legal, tax and record keeping needs.

Your choices

  • Our newsletters include an unsubscribe link, and you can reply to any email from us to ask us to stop.
  • To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email info@thesocialdm.com.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use the Google Analytics opt out. The site still works without them.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no website or email system is completely secure.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@thesocialdm.com.
