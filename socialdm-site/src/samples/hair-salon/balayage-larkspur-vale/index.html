Who it suits

Anyone who wants dimension and a softer grow out than traditional highlights. Your stylist will tell you if it fits your hair and goals.

What to expect

At your consultation your stylist looks at your hair, talks through your goal and gives you a time and price. Results depend on your starting color and hair health.

Questions people ask

How long does balayage last?

Many people go a few months between appointments. It depends on your hair and color.

Will balayage damage my hair?

Any lightening affects hair. Your stylist plans bond care and the right approach for your hair.

Can I go from dark to blonde in one visit?

Sometimes it takes more than one visit. Your stylist will explain the plan.
