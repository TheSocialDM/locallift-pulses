Questions

What sellers ask first

How do you price a home in Larkspur Vale?

Adrienne compares the most recent sales near you, adjusts for size, condition and view, and walks you through every home she used so the number makes sense.

How long does it take to sell?

In this sample market the median home sold in 21 days. Price, condition and season move that number, and you see the recent comparisons before you list.

Do I need to stage my home?

Not always. You get a room-by-room list of what is worth doing and what is not.

What does it cost to sell?

Commission and closing costs are explained in writing before you sign anything. Commissions are negotiable.

Can you sell my home privately?

Yes, where MLS rules allow. Some sellers prefer a quiet, off-market sale to a short list of qualified buyers first.
