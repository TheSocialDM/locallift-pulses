Questions

Buying in Heron Point

How much do homes in Heron Point cost?

Over the last 12 months the median sale was about $2.1M in this sample. Waterfront homes with docks sell at the top of that range.

Do all Heron Point homes have lake access?

Most lots have a private dock or deeded access. Interior streets may not, so check each listing.

Are there rules about docks and boats?

The lake association sets dock and wake rules. Ask for the current rules before you make an offer.

School boundaries change. Confirm with the school district before you buy.
