Real answers

Questions people ask before they book

Plain answers to what people ask before their first visit.

Is the first visit just a consultation?

Yes. Your first visit is a conversation about your goals with a licensed provider. You can choose to be treated the same day, but you never have to.

How long do results from Botox last?

For most people, about three to four months. Your provider will tell you what to expect for your own plan. Results vary from person to person.

Who performs the treatments?

Licensed medical providers working under a medical director. You will always know who is treating you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Ask at your consult. Pricing is always given to you in writing before treatment starts.
