Who it suits

People who want softer forehead lines, frown lines or crow's feet, with a natural look as the goal. It is not right for everyone, which is why every plan starts with a consult. Results vary from person to person.

What to expect

You talk through your goals, your provider maps where to treat, and the treatment itself takes minutes. We check in two weeks later to see how it settled.

Like any medical treatment, Botox has possible side effects, such as bruising or headache. Your provider will go over them, and whether it is right for you, at your consult.

Botox questions people ask

How long does Botox last?

For most people about three to four months. Results vary.

Does Botox hurt?

Most people describe a quick pinch. Your provider can use ice or numbing cream if you want it.

What is the difference between Botox and Dysport?

Both relax expression lines. Your provider will explain which fits your goals at your consult.
