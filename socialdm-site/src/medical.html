How practices rank on Google Maps

Where you show up depends on where the patient is standing.

A practice can be first in Maps a block away and invisible a mile out. Google says local results come down to three things, and patients add a fourth: trust.

  • RelevanceHow well your profile matches what the patient searched.
  • DistanceHow far the patient is from your practice.
  • ProminenceHow well known your practice is, including your reviews and rating.
  • TrustRecent reviews, real photos, accurate hours and clear provider pages.
Source: Google Business Profile Help, “Tips to improve your local ranking”

Do you write medical advice or clinical content?

No. We write about your practice, services, providers and how to book. Clinical statements come from your team, and we ask a clinician to approve them before anything is published.

How do you handle patient privacy in reviews?

Replies stay general. We do not confirm that a reviewer is a patient or mention any care details, and we flag reviews that need a response from your office instead.

Can you work with multiple locations?

Yes. Each location gets its own profile checks and reporting, with one plan across the group.
