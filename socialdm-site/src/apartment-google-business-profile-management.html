FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How often do you post to our Google Business Profile?

Every day, seven days a week. Each community gets a new Google post at 7 AM local time, with current floor plans featured twice a week.

Do you write and publish our Google review replies?

Yes. We write a reply to every Google review, positive or negative, and publish it to your profile the same day the review comes in.

What happens with a negative review?

We write a calm, professional reply. When a review needs details only your team has, such as a maintenance request or a move out question, we flag it to you before the reply goes live.

Can we hire you for just one apartment community?

Yes. Every package is priced per property, so a single community gets the same daily posting, review replies and listing checks as our largest portfolios.

Do we keep ownership of our Google Business Profile?

Yes. Your company stays the primary owner of the profile and adds The SocialDM as a Manager, which covers posts, review replies and profile updates.

Can you promote our current specials on Google?

Yes. Specials go out as Google offer posts with your dates and terms, in line with Google’s posting rules. Your daily posts focus on the community, the homes and the neighborhood.

Where do you work?

We currently manage apartment communities in Arizona, Nevada, Florida, Oregon and Washington, and we take on communities nationwide.
