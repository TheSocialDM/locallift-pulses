FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How often do you post to our Google Business Profile?

Often enough to keep your profile active and current. LocalLift Daily Presence keeps fresh, local posts going out for your community, planned for each property.

Do you write and publish our Google review replies?

Yes. Through LocalLift Review Desk we write a reply to every Google review, positive or negative, and publish it to your profile promptly.

What happens with a negative review?

We write a calm, professional reply. When a review needs details only your team has, such as a maintenance request or a move out question, we flag it to you before the reply goes live.

Can we hire you for just one apartment community?

Yes. Every package is priced per property, so a single community gets the same LocalLift Daily Presence, Review Desk and Site Read work as our largest portfolios.

Do we keep ownership of our Google Business Profile?

Yes. Your company stays the primary owner of the profile and adds The SocialDM as a Manager, which covers posts, review replies and profile updates.

Can you promote our current specials on Google?

Yes. We feature your current specials on your Google profile with your dates and terms, in line with Google’s posting rules.

Where do you work?

Nationwide. The SocialDM markets more than 7,900 apartment homes across Arizona, Nevada, Florida, Oregon and Washington, takes on communities anywhere in the country and is headquartered in the Phoenix and Scottsdale area.
