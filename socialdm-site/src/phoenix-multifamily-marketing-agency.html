Do you only work with Phoenix area apartment communities?

No. The SocialDM markets more than 7,900 apartment homes across five states and is growing nationwide. Our headquarters is in the Phoenix and Scottsdale area, so Valley communities also get an agency that knows the local market firsthand.

Each week it shows how many renters called from your Google listing, asked for directions and clicked through to your website. It gives your onsite and regional teams a regular read on what renters are doing.

We can work on the sources AI tools tend to draw on, such as your Google Business Profile, reviews and social presence. No agency controls how AI tools answer, so we do not promise placement. We focus on keeping those sources strong and reporting what renters do.
