Were those 25 Call button taps (Sep 21 to Oct 18) real calls? Were they answered?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing's number, (888) 867.4501, may be a call tracking number. If it is, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in the property's Yardi call report. Ask for that report filtered to this number.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 868 of 1,085 organic visits (80%) and all 3 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Zillow working?

On the website, Zillow sent 611 visits and 0 leads from September 25 to October 22. Zillow also collects leads inside Zillow, which only Zillow's own report shows. Compare both before renewing.

Why are search appearances down?

From September 24 to October 21, Google showed the site 24% less often, 14,051 appearances against 18,428. Clicks still rose to 687 from 671, because the average position improved to #16.1 and a larger share of searchers clicked. Fewer appearances does not by itself mean fewer renters searched.

Is Google Ads running?

Google Analytics shows 1 visit from Google Ads from September 25 to October 22 and no leads. If there is a Google Ads budget, it sent almost no website visits; check the Google Ads account for the calls and clicks it reports directly.
