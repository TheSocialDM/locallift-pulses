Your questions, answered

Answered from Google's own records
Were those 44 Call button taps real calls? Were they answered?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing's number, (888) 867-4501, may be a call tracking number. If it is, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in the property's Yardi call report. Ask for that report filtered to this number.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 824 of 1,097 organic visits (75%) and 7 of 14 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Zillow working?

On the website, Zillow sent 696 visits and 3 leads in September, one lead per 232 visits. Zillow also collects leads inside Zillow, which only Zillow's own report shows. Compare both before renewing.

Why are search clicks down?

Google showed the site 19% less often. Its average position improved to the best in a year and a larger share of searchers clicked, so the drop came from fewer appearances, not fewer clicks per appearance.

Is Google Ads running?

Google Analytics shows 2 visits from Google Ads in September and no leads. If there is a Google Ads budget, it sent almost no website visits; check the Google Ads account for the calls and clicks it reports directly.
