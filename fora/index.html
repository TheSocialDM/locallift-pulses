Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: similar spend, fewer leads; fix the mix before adding budgetSpend eased 2% ($6,399 to $6,299) while Google Ads website leads fell from 10 to 5, so each lead cost about $1,260 in these four weeks against $640 before. Demand Gen IMA & RMK produced 3 of the 5 leads at $328 each; In Location AI Max spent $2,105 and the outside location search campaign $1,627, with no website leads between them. Ask the ads manager whether those campaigns bring calls or form leads of their own; if not, shift part of their budget toward what produces leads and recheck after two weeks. While homes remain to fill, keep total spend roughly in pace with open homes while cost per lease stays acceptable rather than cutting on habit.

Fix

Other paid sources: ask Zillow for proof before renewing141 visits and no website leads in four weeks, and 128 of those visits arrive with a tag Google files as Unassigned. Zillow collects leads inside Zillow too, so ask for its own lead count and any leases tied to it for October 2 to October 29. If it cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring; Las Vegas may see a milder version of that curve, so watch these monthly lead counts rather than assume. With homes still being advertised, trim the weakest terms and placements in December instead of pausing, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhile many homes are open, keep paid budget steady and point it at the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits. As occupancy rises and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut display, cross network and general search terms first.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a channel only after its cost per lease beats the others, about 15 to 20% at a time, and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Website Oct 2 to Oct 29 · search Oct 1 to Oct 28 · listing Sep 29 to Oct 26, unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 725.226.4438 while the website shows 725.209.4665, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. Answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 725.226.4438.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 559 of 818 organic visits (68%) and 1 of 4 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

It sent 5 of the 9 website leads, one per 464 visits. Spend was $6,299 in these four weeks against $6,399 before, so each website lead cost about $1,260 against $640, as Ads leads fell from 10 to 5. Demand Gen IMA & RMK produced 3 of the 5 at $328 each; In Location AI Max spent $2,105 with none. The Google listing sent 1 lead from 559 visits and 59 Call button taps, with no charge per click. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask the ads manager for those before judging any one campaign.

What moved website leads this period?

Total website leads went from 23 to 9 (Oct 2 to Oct 29 against Sep 4 to Oct 1). Google Ads leads went from 10 to 5 as Ads visits went from 3,133 to 2,318, and Google Ads spend eased 2% ($6,399 to $6,299), so the Google Ads account settings are the place to check what changed. All other sources together went from 13 leads to 4 on 17% fewer visits (2,098 against 2,514); the Leads section has each one.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read of each source and change every Friday: website and leads end October 29, search ends October 28 and the Google listing ends October 26, because Google publishes listing figures about five days late. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (59, Sep 29 to Oct 26) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (13 visits, Oct 2 to Oct 29) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How is Google search trending?

Average position held level at #15.3, the same as the 28 days before, and the share of searchers who clicked eased slightly (11.1% to 10.9%). Clicks eased from 693 to 589 as Google showed the site 13% less often, mostly on searches for your name (504 clicks to 421).
