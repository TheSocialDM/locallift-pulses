Where to put the ad money next

GA4 · our read

Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: same spend, fewer leads; fix the mix before adding budgetSpend held steady ($6,248 against $6,296) while Google Ads website leads fell from 21 to 9, so each lead cost about $694 in these four weeks against $300 before. Demand Gen IMA & RMK produced 8 of the 9 leads at $119 each; In Location AI Max spent $2,078 for 1 lead, and the two outside location campaigns spent $1,551 with none. Separately, 365 Ads visits came from Los Angeles. Ask the ads manager whether those campaigns bring calls or form leads of their own; if not, shift part of their budget toward what produces leads and recheck after two weeks. The website still advertises immediate move ins and a free month, which suggests homes to fill, so keep total spend roughly in pace with open homes while cost per lease stays acceptable rather than cutting on habit.

Fix

Other paid sources: ask Zillow for proof before renewing182 visits and 2 website leads in four weeks, and 167 of those visits arrive with a tag Google files as Unassigned. Zillow collects leads inside Zillow too, so ask for its own lead count and any leases tied to it for September 7 to October 4. If it cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring; Las Vegas may see a milder version of that curve, so watch these monthly lead counts rather than assume. With homes still being advertised, trim the weakest terms and placements in December instead of pausing, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhile many homes are open, keep paid budget steady and point it at the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits. As occupancy rises and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut display, cross network and general search terms first.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a channel only after its cost per lease beats the others, about 15 to 20% at a time, and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 725.226.4438 while the website shows 725.209.4665, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. Answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 725.226.4438.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 687 of 1,062 organic visits (65%) and 5 of 6 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

It sent 9 of the 22 website leads, one per 335 visits. Spend was $6,248 in these four weeks against $6,296 before, so each website lead cost about $694 against $300, as Ads leads fell from 21 to 9. Demand Gen IMA & RMK produced 8 of the 9 at $119 each; In Location AI Max spent $2,078 for 1. The Google listing sent one lead per 137 visits with no charge per click. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask the ads manager for those before judging any one campaign.

What moved website leads this period?

Google Ads leads fell from 21 to 9 as Ads visits fell from 3,812 to 3,011. The Google listing held at 5 and direct leads rose from 2 to 4. Google Ads spend held about level ($6,248 against $6,296), so the Google Ads account settings are the place to check what changed.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read and change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (43) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (37 visits) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How is Google search trending?

Average position improved by 3 places (#17.9 to #14.9) and a slightly larger share of searchers clicked (11.1% to 11.5%). Clicks eased from 879 to 688 as Google showed the site 25% less often, mostly on searches for your name (640 clicks to 494).
