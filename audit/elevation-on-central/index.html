The short version

The site is well built. Its offer and fee details need to agree with each other.

The main headings are working, the plan pages each show their own homes and a fee guide is published. The priority is agreement between pages: the Leasing Offers page and the banner word the offer differently, FAQ fees and pet rent differ from the fee guide, and the FAQ leaves out weekend office hours. No fix changes a working H1 or H2.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /amenities.
What is working
Fee guide is published

The guide lists the redecoration and admin fee, application fee, pet items, parking, late fees and early termination.

Plan pages show their own homes

Each of the 8 plan pages lists its own apartment numbers, with no overlap.

Pet limits are stated

"Max 2 Allowed | Max weight 60 lb each" sits on the amenities page.

Holiday hours are posted ahead

Thanksgiving through New Year closures are listed.
