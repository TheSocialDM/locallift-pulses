The short version

Headings and titles are in good shape. Plan pages and cost details need to catch up.

Main pages have their own titles, the pet policy is detailed and costs are spelled out on the floor plans page. The priority is agreement: fourteen plan pages list home 525 from the A1 plan, the pet deposit shows $200 and $250, the offer page words the special differently, and FAQ monthly fees use other names. No fix changes a working H1 or H2.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /amenities, /floorplans.
