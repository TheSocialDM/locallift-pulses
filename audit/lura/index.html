The short version

The site tells one offer clearly. A few plan pages and fee details need to catch up.

The banner, offer line and popup match on all 25 pages we read, the main headings are doing their job, and the fee guide, cost calculator, pet policy and qualifications are all published. The priority is the detail underneath: the starting one bedroom rent sits on a home available after the move by date, three B1 homes are left out of part of an offer marked valid on all homes, four plan pages list A1 homes, the pet breed lists differ, and the fee guide and cost estimate do not line up with other pages. No fix changes a working H1. The only H2 lines involved are the offer text in F1 and the two video tour labels in F14.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /rentalqualifications, /amenities, /photogallery, /floorplans/one-bedroom-penthouse, /mapsanddirections.
What is working
One special, shown the same on every page

The banner, offer line and popup carry the same offer on all 25 pages, and plan pages repeat the two months free wording per home (three B1 homes differ, see F1)

A published fee guide and cost calculator

Internet $90, amenity $85, trash $25, application fee $75, pet fee $500 and pet rent $50 are listed with one time and monthly labels, plan cards show a total monthly price, and each available home opens a cost estimate

Clear plan facts

Each plan card shows beds, baths, square feet, a starting price and the $500 deposit. The five plans with homes available also show base rent and an availability date

A detailed pet policy

Pet rent, pet fee, three pet limit, no weight restriction and a screening partner are all stated

Specials matching
The offer the site leads with today

“New Home for the Holidays at LURA with 2 Months FREE PLUS Look & Lease Savings Up To $2,000!”

Home page · Popup and floor plans page: "Offer Valid on All Homes & Must Move By 11/25." The top banner itself states no date.

F1Property team

The starting 1 bedroom rent belongs to a home that shows available after the offer's move by date

Home page · /floorplans · /floorplans/a3-one-bedroom · /floorplans/b1-two-bedroom
What it says now
Popup and /floorplans: "Starting Rents With 2 Months Free Applied Over 12-Month Lease: $1,475 = 1 Bed / 1 Bath Homes $2,100 = 2 Bed / 2 Bath Homes Offer Valid on All Homes & Must Move By 11/25." /floorplans/a3-one-bedroom: "Apartment: # 644 Date Available: 1/4/2027" and "1 Apartment Available". /floorplans/b1-two-bedroom, homes 448, 520 and 548: "Please Note That This Home Is Not Eligible For The Additional Look and Lease Savings!"
What needs to change

The $1,475 figure is correct math for the A3 base rent of $1,770, but the only A3 home shows 1/4/2027, after the 11/25 move by date. The 1 bedroom plans that show a date before 11/25 are A7 (card: base rent $1,920, available from 11/13/2026), A1 (home 529: base rent $1,850, 11/22/2026) and the One Bedroom Penthouse (base rent $3,195, available now). The same math on A1 home 529 comes to about $1,542. The $2,100 figure fits B1 homes at $2,520, which show available now. Separately, the popup says the offer is valid on all homes, while the three B1 homes at that $2,520 rent are marked as not eligible for the Look and Lease part. A renter who reads $1,475 and the 11/25 date together finds no home on the site that fits both.
