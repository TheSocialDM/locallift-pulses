The short version

The site carries a lot of good detail. The FAQ and some plan pages need to catch up.

Montage has a complete floor plan list, a published fee guide, a cost calculator and matching pet terms, and its office hours match across the site. The priority is the FAQ, which still shows an offer with a 10/15 end date and says all floor plans are available, plus 15 plan pages that list the same one bedroom apartment. Two items are questions for your team to confirm, not errors. No fix changes a working H1. The only H2 lines involved are the popup title in F10 and the C12 plan name in F3, both after your team confirms.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /amenities, /photogallery, /contactus, /scheduletour, /mapsanddirections.
What is working
Office hours match across the site

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday and Sunday 10 AM to 5 PM appear the same in the footer, the tour page, the contact page and the FAQ.

Complete floor plan list

All 29 plans on the Floor Plans page show beds, baths, square feet and a $400 deposit. The stated range of 686 to 2,281 sq ft matches the smallest plan (One Bedroom B2, 686 sq ft) and the largest (Four Bedroom Townhome D2, 2,281 sq ft).

Pet policy is clear and matches the Fee Guide

"Pet Rent: $35/month", "Maximum Pets Allowed: Up to 3 per home", "No Weight Restrictions" and "Pet Deposit: $300 per pet" appear on the Amenities page and agree with the Fee Guide.

A published Fee Guide

The Fee Guide, linked in the menu, lists the $50 application fee, $400 holding deposit, $400 standard deposit, $300 pet deposit, $35 pet rent, $75 to $180 monthly parking, and late and returned payment fees.
