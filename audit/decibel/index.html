The short version

Search interest is healthy. Hours and plan labels are what renters may trip on.

The main headings are working and the MFTE program has its own clear page. The priority is plan and hours detail: weekend hours differ between the contact and tour pages, nine plan pages list home 701 from another plan, and three income restricted plans carry a Studio, 0 bath label. No fix changes a working H1. The only H2 touched is a spelling correction to Mt. Rainier in F7.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /faq.

F1RentCafe or developer

Weekend office hours conflict between two pages

/contactus · /scheduletour
What it says now
/contactus: "Saturday: 12 AM to - 12 AM Sunday: 12 AM to - 12 AM". /scheduletour: "Saturday: Closed Sunday: Closed"
What needs to change

The contact page shows midnight to midnight on both weekend days, while the schedule a tour page shows both days Closed. A renter cannot tell whether the office is open on the weekend.

Ready to paste
Saturday: Closed. Sunday: Closed. (the property team confirms the weekend hours, then both pages match)
How to do it

Update office hours in the website content tools. If the setting will not change, ask RentCafe.
