The short version

The site is solid. A few FAQ and offer lines have fallen behind the rest of the site.

Plan pages show their own homes, parking and pet costs are published, and the main headings are working. The priority is the FAQ and the offer wording: pet weight, office hours and monthly fees differ from other pages, and the offer button says up to 11 weeks while the popup body says 11 weeks. No fix changes a working H1 or H2.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /photogallery.
