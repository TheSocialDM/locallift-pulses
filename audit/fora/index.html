The short version

Strong foundations. The offer needs one clear version on every page.

Fora publishes a full fee guide, and its banner rents match the plan table. Every plan shows size, beds and baths. The priority is the offer itself: the lowest two bedroom rent sits on homes marked not eligible for additional savings, and the $500 part of the offer is worded differently from page to page. The C1 three bedroom page also lists one bedroom homes. None of the fixes touch a working H1 or H2.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /firstresponder, /preferredemployer, /photogallery, /amenities, /contactus, /mapsanddirections.
What is working
A full Fee Guide is published

The Fee Guide lists the $500 standard deposit, $75 application fee, $300 admin fee, $85 amenity, $60 internet and $25 trash monthly charges, pet fees, garage, carport and storage prices, and usage based utilities.

Banner rents match the plan table

The banner says 1 Bed / 1 Bath = $1718 and 2 Bed / 2 Bath = $1987, which equal the lowest base rents on the floor plans page (A1 $1,718 and B1 $1,987).

Every plan shows size, beds and baths

A1 805 sq ft, A2 876, B1 1,150, B2 1,379, B3 1,349 and C1 1,453. Five plans show a total monthly price with the base rent beside it, and C1 shows Call for details. Sizes match the video tours page wherever a plan appears there.

Programs have their own pages with real numbers

First Responder and Preferred Employer pages state $50 off monthly rent, $600 total annual savings and $375 in credited application and admin fees, which equals the $75 application fee plus $300 admin fee in the Fee Guide.

F1Property team

The lowest two bedroom rent in the offer is on homes marked not eligible for additional savings

Home page · /floorplans/b1-two-bedroom · /floorplans/a1-one-bedroom
What it says now
Banner: "Falling Rates + 1 Month FREE Rent Tour & Apply Same Day for up to $500 More! Base Rents Starting From: 1 Bed / 1 Bath = $1718 2 Bed / 2 Bath = $1987". Specials panel on Apartment 113 and Apartment 150 (base rent $1,987): "REDUCED RENT! Not eligible for additional savings or concessions."
What needs to change

The banner puts the free month and the $1,987 rent in one message, while the two homes at that rent, and Apartment 250 at $1,994, say they are not eligible for additional savings. The A1 homes at $1,718 show no Specials link at all, while the plan card on the Floor Plans page says "Specials Available". A renter can read the banner as $1,987 plus a free month, then learn at the home level that the two do not combine.

Ready to paste
The lowest two bedroom rent shown is a reduced rent. Reduced rent homes are not eligible for additional savings or concessions. See each home for its special.
How to do it

Please confirm the rule first. If it holds, add this line under "Base Rents Starting From" in the banner and popup, in the website content tools. If the free month does apply to these homes, skip the line and remove "Not eligible for additional savings or concessions." from their Specials panels instead. Please also confirm whether the A1 homes at $1,718 receive the free month, and whether First Responder and Preferred Employer savings apply to reduced rent homes. The Specials panels come from the unit special settings, so ask RentCafe or your developer if your team cannot find them.
