The short version

The fee and pricing tools are strong. The older pre opening wording is what needs to go.

Stillwater Lakes has a clear fee guide, a cost estimate that lists costs line by line, and live pricing by building. The priority is older wording that still appears: Maps and Directions mentions late July move ins, the FAQ describes pre opening conditions, and townhome availability differs between pages. Four townhome plan pages also list a home from another plan. No fix changes a working H1. The only H2 lines involved are the Studio S1 tour label in F11 and the hard hat tour question in F3, if your team removes it.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /photogallery, /amenities, /mapsanddirections, /contactus, /faq, /interactivepropertymap.
What is working
Clear fee guide

The fee guide lists Admin Fee $300, Application Fee $80, Internet $89, Trash Services $36, Smart Home Tech $30, Pest Control $6, Pet Fee $200 and Pet Rent $20, and says when each is due.

Cost estimate lists costs line by line

Calculate My Costs on a plan shows Application Cost, Move in Cost and Monthly Cost by line. Studio S1 shows base rent $1,481 and a Total Monthly Leasing Price of $1,642, which is base rent plus the four monthly fees in the fee guide.

Full rental qualifications are published

The Rental Qualifications page gives income, guarantor, rental history, credit, criminal history, occupancy and pet requirements, marked Effective 03.17.26.

Pet costs and limits are stated

Pet Rent: $20/month per pet, Maximum Pets Allowed: Up to 3 per home, No Weight Restrictions: All sizes welcome, Pet Fee: $200 per pet, non refundable, with a named breed list.
