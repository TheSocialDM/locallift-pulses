The short version

One offer runs across the whole site. The details behind it need a tune up.

COYA runs one offer across every page we read, publishes an itemized fee guide, and shows a total monthly price on each plan card with homes available. The priority is what sits just behind that: the lowest two bedroom price in the offer is on a home marked not eligible for savings, older move in wording still appears, the studio page lists one bedroom homes, and fee and pet details differ between pages. No fix changes a working H1. The only H2 lines involved are two corrections in F8 (the maps page spelling and the 24/Hour Fitness tile) and the video tour labels in F7.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /amenities, /photogallery, /firstresponderappreciation, /preferredemployer, /mapsanddirections, /interactivepropertymap, /contactus, /videotours.
What is working
A clear, itemized fee guide

The fee guide lists the $500 standard deposit (on approved credit), $300 admin fee, $75 application fee, internet $90, amenity $85, trash $25 and utility billing $4 per month, pet deposit $300 and pet rent $50, plus NSF and late fees, with an Email PDF option.

Plan cards show a total monthly price

Eight of the nine plan cards show base rent and a Total Monthly Leasing Price (studio plan E1 says "Call for details"). For A1 the base rent of $1,460.00 plus $204.00 in required monthly fees equals the $1,664.00 shown, and the $204 matches the fee guide (internet, amenity, trash, utility billing).

Offer prices match the plan table

The popup and the floor plans page say 1BR $1460, 2BR $1945, 3BR $2835. The lowest base rents in the plan table are $1,460 (A1), $1,945 (B3) and $2,835 (C2).

One offer across every page read

The banner and popup were found on all 33 pages read, with no old seasonal offer left in any title, meta description, popup or banner.

“New Home for the Holidays at COYA with Falling Rents + Up to $2000 Look & Lease Bonus + Stack 1st Responder or Preferred Employer Special Discounts!”

What does it cost to move in and what are the monthly fees?The fee guide lists a $500 standard deposit (on approved credit), a $300 admin fee and a $75 application fee per applicant. Are pets allowed at COYA?Yes. Pet rent is $50 per month per pet and the pet deposit is $300 per pet. Up to 3 pets are allowed per home with no weight restrictions. Some breed restrictions apply and pets are registered through PetScreening. (Your team confirms the breed list, see F5.)Source: /amenities
Do you offer discounts for first responders, military, teachers or employees of partner employers?Yes. First Responder Appreciation offers $100 off monthly rent, a $99 deposit and waived application and admin fees (on approved credit) for firefighters, police officers, EMTs, active duty military and teachers. The Preferred Employer program offers $50 off monthly rent, a $99 deposit and waived application and admin fees worth $375 (on approved credit). See the Preferred Employer page for who qualifies.Source: /firstresponderappreciation
What do I need to qualify to rent?Combined gross monthly income must be 2.5 times the rent, with the two most recent pay stubs. Guarantors must earn 4 times the rent and can help with income only. Applicants complete credit and criminal background screening. Maximum occupancy is 3 persons for a one bedroom, 5 for a two bedroom and 7 for a three bedroom.Source: /rentalqualifications
