      Five questions to ask Birdeye before signing anything

      1. What is the price per community, and the onboarding fee per community? Third party reviews report $299 to $449 per location per month, plus an estimated $500 to $1,500 per location to onboard.
      2. What are the term, the cancellation notice and the renewal fee? Third party pricing reviews report 12 month auto renewing contracts, 90 days written notice, and a renewal "Innovation Fee" of about 8%.
      3. Who does the work each week? Software that flags a wrong fact still needs someone to fix the website, the listing and the Google profile.
      4. How are review requests sent? Google's policy does not allow asking only satisfied residents for reviews. One of Birdeye's own apartment case studies describes routing unsatisfied residents to management and satisfied ones to review sites. Ask how their setup stays inside Google's rules.
      5. Which AI engines and how many questions are included? Their AI search product is billed by the number of questions checked, per community, per engine, and by how often they run.

      What we recommend next

      1. Add Cuvee and Huxley to our weekly AI Shortlist and fact tracking. Each week we would log what ChatGPT and Gemini say about each community, check every fact against the website, and flag anything that drifts. This is the same program our LocalLift communities have.
      2. Listing accuracy cleanup. One pass to bring the specials, rents, pet rules and phone numbers on the major listing sites in line with each website, then a weekly watch.
      3. Close the Huxley gap. Huxley shows on ChatGPT's map for North Scottsdale but not in its written picks. Our answer coverage work puts specific, consistent facts everywhere AI reads, which improves the odds Huxley is named in the written answer. No one can guarantee placement.
