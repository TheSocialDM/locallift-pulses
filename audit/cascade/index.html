The short version

The offer is clear across the site. The pet and fee answers need attention.

The offer reads the same on the pages read live, the main headings are working and a fee guide and Pricing Guide are published. The priority is one plan page and a few FAQ answers: the B4 page lists an A1 home, the pet friendly answer repeats the parking answer, pet fees read opposite to the guides, and monthly fees differ between the FAQ, the Pricing Guide and the fee guide. No fix changes a working H1 or H2.

Keep these main headings as they are. They read clearly and the pages are earning search visibility: Home page, /floorplans, /photogallery.

F3Property team

FAQ pet fees read opposite to the fee guide

/faq · /feeoverview · /floorplans
What it says now
/faq: "$200 non-refundable fee / $225 refundable deposit". /feeoverview: "Pet Deposit Per Pet $200.00 - $500.00", "Pet Deposit - Refundable", "Pet Fee Per Pet $225.00", and "Pet Rent Per Pet $30.00 Per Month". The Pricing Guide on /floorplans: "Pet Fee: $225 for 1 pet, $325 for 2 pets / Deposit: $200.00 first pet, $500 for 2 pets"
What needs to change

The FAQ calls $200 the fee and $225 the deposit. The fee guide and the Pricing Guide show the reverse, and the FAQ leaves out pet rent. Renters need to know which part comes back to them.

Ready to paste
Pet fee: $225 for one pet and $325 for two pets. Refundable deposit: $200 for the first pet and $500 for two pets. Pet rent: $30 per pet per month, up to two pets. (the property team confirms)

Confirm the amounts, then edit the FAQ answer.
