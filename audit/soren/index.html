The short version

The site tells the Ballard story well. Offer wording, hours and plan labels need a look.

The site leads with Ballard, publishes a fee guide and states plan sizes clearly. The priority is three things renters see today: the popup words the $1000 offer differently, Sunday hours read as midnight to midnight, and the headings promise studios while the plan list labels open layouts as one bedrooms. Search data is not connected for this site, so no heading is judged on clicks. The extra slogan heading and empty H1 tag in F4 are a template cleanup, and the studio wording in F3 changes only after the property team confirms.

What is working
Ballard is front and center

The site opens on Ballard living and the footer places the building near Ballard parks and main corridors.

Fee guide is published

The guide lists application fee $14.00, pet rent $45.00 and early termination.

Plan sizes are clear

"Square footage is listed as actual livable square footage, not including patios or storage"

Holiday hours are posted ahead

Upcoming holiday hours are listed on the contact page.

Fix now

3 fixes renters can see today

F1Property team

The popup words the offer differently from the offer line

Home page · /leasingoffers
What it says now
Offer line: "$1000 Off Move in *Ask for details, select homes and terms". Popup: "Move in Special! Limited time offer $1000 off rent! * Ask for details, select homes and terms only."
What needs to change

Off move in and off rent can mean different things to a renter. A renter may read a one time move in credit as a rent reduction.

Ready to paste
(the wording the property team confirms, the same on the offer line, popup and Leasing Offers page)
How to do it

Confirm how the $1000 applies, then update the popup.

F3Property team

Headings promise studios, and the plan list shows none

Home page · /floorplans
What it says now
Home H1: "Soren Apartments - Studio, 1 & 2 Bedroom Homes in Ballard, Seattle". /floorplans H2: "Studio, One & Two-Bedroom Apartments in Ballard, Seattle". The plan filter offers "1 Bedroom" and "2 Bedrooms", and open layouts show as "oA7 - Open" with "1 Bed".
What needs to change

The open layouts may be what the site means by studio, but the plan list labels them as one bedrooms. A renter searching for a studio finds no plan labeled that way.

Ready to paste
