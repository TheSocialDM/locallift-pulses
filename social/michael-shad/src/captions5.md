Can AI repeat your special correctly?

"Huge savings! Weeks free on select homes! Call for details, restrictions apply" might stop a scroll, but it's hard for people, or AI tools, to repeat accurately.

Compare it to: 6 weeks free on 1 bedroom homes with a 12 month lease, move in by November 30. Clear terms, a clear end date, nothing to guess. (Example special only, not a real offer.)

When a renter asks an AI tool about deals near them, vague wording is more likely to get skipped or mangled. Clear wording has a better chance of being passed along the way you meant it.

Two habits help: write each special the same way on your website, Google profile and listing sites, and take it down everywhere the day it ends.

If it only lives in an image, AI may not read it.

Many property websites show floor plans as a picture: a drawing with the plan name, size and features baked into the image. It looks fine to a person. But text inside an image is harder for search and AI tools to read reliably. Tools can often tell what a photo shows, but small print like square footage is easy to miss or misread.

The fix is easy. Under each floor plan image, add the key details as real text: plan name, beds and baths, square footage, standout features and pet policy.

Same goes for specials shown only as a banner graphic, and amenity lists that only appear inside photos.
