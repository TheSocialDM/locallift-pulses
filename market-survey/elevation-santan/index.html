Terms used in this report

Asking rent: the advertised monthly rent renters see online.
Effective rent (NER): what a resident really pays per month after the special is spread over a 12 month lease.
Special / concession %: how much the move in deal takes off a year of rent.
Leased %: share of homes that are leased.
Exposure %: share of homes open to lease now or soon. Higher means more to fill.
Market rent: what similar homes nearby are asking.
Days on market: how long a home has been listed without leasing.
Leases per week: new leases signed, counted from live listings.
Weeks to lease: homes available now divided by leases per week.
Review rating: average resident review score across the review sites in the data, out of 5.
Rivals: the 20 nearby communities this report compares Elevation SanTan to.
Rent per sq ft: monthly rent divided by the home's size, to compare different sized homes fairly.
