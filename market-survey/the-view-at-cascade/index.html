Terms used in this report

Asking rent: the advertised monthly rent renters see online.
Effective rent (NER): what a resident really pays per month after the special is spread over a 12 month lease.
Special / concession %: how much the move in deal takes off a year of rent.
Leased %: share of homes that are leased.
Exposure %: share of homes open to lease now or soon. Higher means more to fill.
Market rent: what similar homes nearby are asking.
Days on market: how long a home has been listed without leasing.
Leases per week: new leases signed, counted from live listings.
Weeks to lease: homes available now divided by leases per week.
Renewal %: estimated share of residents who renew their lease.
Rivals: the 20 nearby communities this report compares Cascade to.
Rent per sq ft: monthly rent divided by the home's size, to compare different sized homes fairly.

Who is beating Cascade, and how

How we picked these rivals

  1. We looked at every community Cascade competes with: the 20 nearby rentals in the market data, plus any we know renters tour that the data missed, like Camden North End.
  2. We asked one question: who is signing the leases Cascade is not? Every community is ranked by leases signed per week, counted the same way for all of them, so the ranking is fair.
  3. The top 5 are where Cascade's renters are ending up. Each is signing 3.8 to 4.6 leases a week, about four times Cascade's pace, and each is over 95% leased.
  4. Camden North End was added by hand. It is about a mile away, newer, priced about 7% lower and has only 3% of its homes open. It appears to be leasing well, even though the data source left it off the list.
  5. Everyone else is leasing slower, so they are not taking renters from Cascade right now. They are listed below so you can see we checked them. The list is rechecked every week, and any rival that speeds up moves into the top 5.
