How to read this report

  1. Leasing pace. Is Cascade winning renters? About 2.3 leases a week vs 3 to 4 at similar comps. Slower.
  2. Leased % and exposure. How big is the risk? 90.9% leased, 12.3% exposure. Weakest within 1.5 miles.
  3. Price check. Price problem or marketing problem? 1BRs are at market, so it is marketing. B3 and B4 are priced high.
  4. Floor plan comps. Which homes are off? A1 and B3 are above market and sitting longest. B2 is below market.
  5. Cost to move in. $350 in move in fees, the highest in the comp set.
  6. Who is taking the renters. San Milan, Scottsdale Grand and One North Scottsdale. Scottsdale Grand is advertising 6 weeks free plus a waived admin fee.

Price check

Asking rent is the advertised price on the website and ILSs, the number renters filter by. Effective rent (NER) is what a resident actually pays once the concession is spread over a 12 month lease.

1BR asking rent is at market. 2BR asking rent reads above market because of B3 and B4. B1 and B2 are at or below market. Fix B3 pricing, not every 2BR.
