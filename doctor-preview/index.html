Questions

The short answers.

Do you handle patient information?
No. We work only with referring doctors and their offices. Patient records stay in your systems.
Who gets the replies?
Your office. Replies come straight to you and your team.
Do you guarantee referrals?
No. Referrals are always the referring doctor's decision. Our job is to keep you thoughtfully in touch.
Is there new software for my staff?
No. Nothing to install and nothing to learn. Your team keeps working the way it does today.
