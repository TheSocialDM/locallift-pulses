The questions people are already asking.

Sound familiar?

Good relationships fade quietly.

None of this is anyone's fault. It is just what happens when a practice gets busy.

New faces at the front desk

Referring offices change staff often. The new office manager may not know your name yet.

Details that drift

An old suite number, an old practice name or a different phone number can sit online for years.

Quiet offices go unnoticed

When an office stops calling, nobody sees it until a month feels slow.

No time for thank you notes

Between clinic and the OR, the personal notes that build trust are the first thing to slip.

Questions

The short answers.

Do you handle patient information?
No. We work only with referring doctors and their offices. Patient records stay in your systems.
Who gets the replies?
Your office. Replies come straight to you and your team.
Do you guarantee referrals?
No. Referrals are always the referring doctor's decision. Our job is to keep you thoughtfully in touch.
Is there new software for my staff?
No. Nothing to install and nothing to learn. Your team keeps working the way it does today.
