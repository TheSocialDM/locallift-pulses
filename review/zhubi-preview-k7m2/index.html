Clinical tip

When to consider a colorectal surgery referral

  • Rectal bleeding that persists or is unexplained
  • A change in bowel habits lasting several weeks
  • Hemorrhoids not improving with conservative care
  • Recurrent or complicated diverticulitis
  • An anal fissure or fistula that is not healing
  • A polyp or finding that needs a surgical opinion

Answer what patients are already asking

Questions like these often appear on colonoscopy searches. Each one becomes a clear answer under your name.

Who is the best doctor for a colonoscopy?
No single doctor is best for everyone. Compare training, how often the doctor performs the procedure, patient reviews and how clearly the office explains each step.
How much does a colonoscopy typically cost in Arizona?
It depends on your insurance, where it is done and whether polyps are removed. Call the office before your visit so the team can help you check your plan.
Which one is more painful, an endoscopy or a colonoscopy?
Both are usually done with sedation, so many people feel little during the procedure. Many say the colonoscopy prep is the hardest part. Ask your doctor what to expect.
Which specialist should I see for a colonoscopy?
Gastroenterologists and colorectal surgeons both perform colonoscopies. Your primary care doctor can help you decide, especially if surgery may be part of the conversation.

Questions

The short answers.

Which plans include Referral Desk?
Annual plans include it. Because you are a founding practice, Referral Desk is included with your annual plan, a $495 a month value, whether you start on your own or with your partners. On a 6 month plan it is a $495 a month add on for the whole practice. It is not offered month to month. See the offer.
Is this a CRM we have to run?
No. We do the job a CRM is supposed to do, without you running one. There is nothing to log in to, and your list is always yours. Ask for a copy any time.
Do you handle patient information?
No. We never receive patient records or contact patients. Patient records stay in your systems.
Who gets the replies?
Your office. Replies come straight to you and your team.
Do you guarantee referrals?
No. Referrals are always the referring doctor's decision. Our job is to keep you thoughtfully in touch.
Is there new software for my staff?
No. Nothing to install and nothing to learn. Your team keeps working the way it does today.
Will we get more business right away?
We can't promise that. Referral relationships build over time. In the first 30 days, you can see what went out, who replied and who to call.
Couldn't my staff or an AI tool do this?
They could write a letter. The hard part is doing it all year, keeping the list current and noticing who has gone quiet, while your team stays focused on patients.
What if we decide to stop?
Your list stays yours. We hand it back. We would rather keep you because the work is useful.
Who will actually see this?
The doctors and office managers on your list, in their inbox. Patients find your answer pages through search and your profile.
How do offices find your answer pages?
They don't have to. Every email links to it, and so does your referral card.
My list is small. Is that a problem?
No. Even 30 good offices is a strong start, and we add new ones as you meet them.
We don't have a list. What then?
Your staff can pull recent referring offices from your system, using names and offices only, with no patient information.
Will doctors think this is spam?
Each note is short, useful and from you. You approve it, and anyone can unsubscribe.
Is this just AI writing?
AI helps us draft and organize. We review it, and nothing goes out until you approve it.
We already have someone helping with SEO.
That can continue. Referral care is about the offices that already know you, and we can work alongside anyone you use.
Does this replace my hospital's marketing?
No. It builds your own name and relationships, alongside anything your hospital does.
Can my partners join later?
Yes. Start on your own and add Dr. Lipton and Dr. Villareal when it makes sense. Referral Desk covers the whole practice on one list, and partners who join within 6 months get their setup at half price.
