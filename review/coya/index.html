Strategic read

Opportunity & brand risk

The opportunity

Own personal, secure, remote-worker living.

AI credits Coya for high-touch leasing and maintenance compared with corporate landlords, smart security with gated entry and package lockers, and a modern new build that suits people working from home. Those are the stories to publish as independent proof, on review and renter sites, so AI starts naming Coya in comparison answers.

The brand risk

A shared name, fees, and screening.

AI sometimes confuses Coya with a digital insurance company of the same name, and "koya" searches show up too. It also flags parking and towing rules as rigid, application screening as strict, and upfront fees as high against space. Using "Coya Apartments Las Vegas" the same way everywhere and publishing clear total cost and screening criteria protects the brand.
