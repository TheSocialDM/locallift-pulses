Plain English

Three words this report uses, and what each one means

These three measures come up throughout. Here is what each one actually is, how it works, and what we are doing about it for Village.

Share of voice
WHAT IT MEANS
Out of all the times a renter is shown apartment options, the slice that is you. Picture 100 renters looking: share of voice is how many of those 100 see your name.
HOW IT WORKS
We measure it on two separate surfaces, and they are different numbers. Map share is your slice of the Google map across the whole city. AI share of voice is how often an AI assistant names you when asked where to rent in Chandler.
WHAT WE DO ABOUT IT
Keep the profile active and readable so Google keeps showing you, and publish content AI can quote so it names you more often.
Where Village stands: 27% of the Chandler map, nearly double the next community. 4% of AI answers, and the only community in the city that rose this cycle.
Citations
WHAT IT MEANS
Any page on the internet an AI reads about you and treats as a source. A listing page, a review, a forum thread, a news mention, or a page on your own site. It is not a link anyone clicks.
HOW IT WORKS
AI does not invent recommendations, it repeats what it has read. If your own pages are not among the sources, AI describes you in a listing site's words, and that site lists your competitors on the same page.
WHAT WE DO ABOUT IT
Publish answers to the questions renters actually ask, on your pages, labelled so machines can read them. Every one becomes another source in your voice.
Where Village stands: 205 sources name Village. The most-cited source in the Chandler market has 495. This is the most directly buildable number in the report.
Impressions
WHAT IT MEANS
One time your website appeared in Google's search results, whether or not anyone clicked it. A click is when someone actually chose you.
HOW IT WORKS
Impressions show how often Google is willing to put you in front of a renter. Clicks show whether they picked you. A wide gap between the two means one thing: you are appearing, but too far down the page to be reached.
WHAT WE DO ABOUT IT
Rewrite and structure the pages that already appear, so the searches sitting on page two move up to page one where renters actually click.
Where Village stands: appeared 42,573 times, clicked 1,360 times. In the top three positions 47% of people click. At positions 11 to 20, under 2% do. The visibility already exists, the position is what is missing.
The one sentence version

Share of voice is how often renters see you. Citations are what AI has read about you. Impressions are how often Google offered you up. Village is strong on the first, building the second, and sitting on a large amount of the third that is not yet converting because of position.

Opportunity & brand risk

The opportunity

Own the house-like, pet-forward, quiet-living niche.

AI recognises Village for private enclosed backyards, casita-style layouts, smart-home tech and genuine pet-friendliness, with 45+ mentions on house-like living. Renters ask for exactly this: townhome-style rentals, enclosed yards for pets, house-like living with privacy. Package it as a flagged “Village Homes” collection and export that language to every listing, page and post to turn a recognised strength into share of voice.

The brand risk

Pricing perception & a split brand name.

Pricing, fees & value is where AI sentiment softens, renters flag premium rents and mandatory tech/service add-ons (~$117 in extras). AI also splits the brand between “Village of Chandler” and “the Village of Chandler,” diluting a combined 4.83% share of voice. A “no-surprises” pricing story and one standardised name are the highest-leverage fixes this cycle.
