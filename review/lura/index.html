Opportunity & brand risk

The opportunity

Own the secure Strip basecamp.

AI already credits Lura for Strip-adjacent location, easy commutes, controlled access with gated garage parking, and a 2024 build. No rival leads on that combination. Say it the same way everywhere, on the site, the profile and the listings, and give AI more pages worth citing on luxury and Strip questions. That is how a location lead becomes a share-of-voice lead.

The brand risk

Fees, noise, and the Aura name.

AI flags layered fees against unit size and some noise between homes as the areas to improve. Separately, the old name still travels: 148 August search clicks came from people typing "aura". Those renters do land on the site, because the old address forwards, but it forwards temporarily, so the history behind it is not being handed over. Publishing clear total cost and finishing the rename across the site and listings protects the 93% sentiment.
