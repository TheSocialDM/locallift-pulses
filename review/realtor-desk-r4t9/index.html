    Answers to what your market is asking

    Questions like these often come up on home searches. Each one can become a clear answer under your name.

    How do I find out what my home is worth?
    Online estimates are a starting point. A local agent can compare recent nearby sales and look at the condition of your home, usually for free.
    What does it cost to sell a house in Arizona?
    Costs usually include agent commission, which is negotiable, title and escrow fees, and any repairs or prep. Ask for a written estimate of what you would take home.
    Is it better to buy first or sell first?
    It depends on your finances and how tight your local market is. Talk with your lender and your agent before you decide.
    How do I choose a real estate agent?
    Ask friends who they used, check recent sales in your area, read reviews and meet with more than one. Pick the agent who explains things clearly.

    Sample answers. General information only, and you approve every word before anything is published.

    Questions

    The short answers.

    Do I have to learn new software?
    No. There is nothing to log in to. You send names to us, and your Monday Sheet comes by email.
    My list is a mess. Is that a problem?
    No. Sorting it out is part of the job. We email only people you know, and you can tell us who to skip.
    I only have about 100 contacts.
    That is a fine start. These are people who already know you.
    Who do the emails come from?
    You. They carry your name, your email address, your brokerage and your office address.
    Who gets the replies?
    You do. Replies come to your inbox. We see a copy so your Monday Sheet stays accurate, and you get a copy of everything your people receive.
    Do I approve every email?
    You approve your first month and how it sounds. After that it runs, so it keeps moving on your busy weeks. Change, skip or pause anything, any time.
    How many emails will people get?
    One or two a week at most, and each group gets its own set. Anyone can unsubscribe, and we remove them for you.
    Will this get me more deals?
    We can't promise that. It keeps you in front of people who already know you. Once emails start, you can see what went out, who replied and who to call.
    I already have a CRM.
    Keep it if it works for you, and send us an export. If yours never got fully set up, we run the part that keeps getting skipped.
    Couldn't I do this with an email tool or AI?
    You could write an email. The hard part is doing it every week for years, keeping the list current and noticing who just raised a hand.
    What about my brokerage's rules?
    Every email carries your brokerage name and office address, and copy is checked for Fair Housing language. Your broker can review the samples before anything goes out.
    Do you send texts or print?
    No. This is email only, and you can print any card on your own if you like.
    Is this just AI writing?
    AI helps us draft and organize. We review it, and you approve how it sounds before anything starts.
    Is my list safe? Whose list is it?
    Your list stays yours. We use it only to run your emails. Ask for your database any time, and we hand it back if you stop.
