Strategic read

Opportunity & brand risk

The opportunity

Own the new-build, work-from-home story.

AI credits Fora for modern new-build finishes with a hotel-like feel, plus lounges, fitness and pools that suit remote professionals. Renters are searching for it: "new apartments las vegas" and "brand new apartments in las vegas" showed Fora 220 times in August and earned no clicks. Pages built for those searches turn a recognised strength into visits.

The brand risk

The name, noise, and billing.

A search for just "fora" puts the site at position 3.6, so other brands sharing the name still compete, and "fora apartments henderson" ranks 10.6. AI also flags noise and density concerns and a clunky billing experience after move-in. Using "Fora Apartments Las Vegas" the same way everywhere, and addressing billing and noise in resident communication, protects the 100% sentiment.
