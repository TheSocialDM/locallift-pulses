What October showed

  • The momentum held after launch

    Every October week cleared 220 impressions. Before the program, a full week drew 8.

  • The right industry is following

    Personal care product manufacturing is now 21% of Renee's audience, up from 13% at launch. It is the largest industry in her audience by a wide margin.

  • Decision makers are following

    CXO, Director and Owner level members now make up 42% of her audience. These are the roles that typically approve new products and partnerships.

  • The reach went national

    Phoenix fell from 38% to 32% of her audience, and New York (20%) and Los Angeles (10%) are now her next largest markets. Among people who saw her posts in this period, New York ranked first at 13%.

  • Conversation is the next step

    The reach is in place. The next step is turning it into comments and replies from the right people.

Alongside the content program, we added hands on reputation and relationship work to Renee's LinkedIn this month. Reach shows who sees her posts. This work is aimed at turning those viewers into real connections.
