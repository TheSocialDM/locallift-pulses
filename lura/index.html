Where to put the ad money next

Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 vs Aug 10 to Sep 6
GA4 · our read

Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: move budget from campaigns with no leads to the one that convertsSpend rose 24% ($4,592 to $5,696) while website leads went from 9 to 7, so each lead cost $814 against $510 before. Demand Gen sent 5 of the 7 leads for $951, about $190 each. The In Location and Outside Location campaigns spent more than $2,700 together with no website leads, and Outside Location grew from $246 to $1,211. They may still drive calls the ads account counts, so ask the Google Ads manager for that report before cutting, then shift budget toward Demand Gen and recheck cost per lead in two weeks. The Display campaign cost $106 and brought no website leads.

Fix

Other paid sources: ask Zillow, Apartments.com and Apartment List for proof before renewingZillow sent 312 website visits and 1 lead; Apartments.com and Apartment List sent 16 visits and none. These sites collect leads inside their own pages, so ask each for its lead count and any leases tied to it for September 7 to October 4. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring, and Las Vegas may run on its own rhythm, so your own months are the better guide: leads ran 54 in April and 74 in June. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhen occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many units come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits. Google Ads brought 45% of all visits and 17% of leads, so visits alone are a poor guide here.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level. No paid channel here has earned that yet: Google Ads gave one lead per 390 visits against one per 92 from the listing. If the campaign cleanup brings Google Ads closer to the listing's rate, scale it in those steps.

Scale

The listing remains your most efficient large source: one website lead per 92 visits against one per 390 from Google Ads, plus 56 Call button taps, with no charge per click.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing's number, 702.941.7223, is different from the website's 702.935.6015, so it is likely the property's call tracking number. If it is, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that call tracking report. Ask for it filtered to this number.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 1,104 of 1,440 organic visits (77%) and 12 of 16 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

On the website, Google Ads sent 2,729 visits and 7 leads, one lead per 390 visits. All 7 came from cross network campaigns; Display sent 984 visits and Search ads 347, with no leads from either. Against the four weeks before, visits rose 15% (2,369 to 2,729) while leads went from 9 to 7. Spend rose 24%, from $4,592 to $5,696, so each website lead cost $814 against $510 before. Demand Gen sent 5 of the 7 leads; the two location search campaigns spent more than $2,700 with none. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask for that report before judging the search campaigns. See Google Ads by campaign.

How is search trending?

Average position improved from #7.7 to #6.1. Clicks were 809 against 1,099 in the four weeks before as Google showed the site 22% less often, and clicks on searches for Lura by name fell from 491 to 366. The change appears to come from fewer appearances, not a lower ranking.

Is Zillow working?

On the website, Zillow sent 312 visits and 1 lead, one lead per 312 visits. 269 of those visits carry a Zillow tag that GA4 files as Unassigned. Zillow also collects leads inside Zillow, which only Zillow's own report shows. Compare both before renewing.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read and change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (56) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (27 visits) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.
