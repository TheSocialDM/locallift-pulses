Fix

Other paid sources: ask Zillow, Apartments.com and Apartment List for proof before renewingZillow sent 213 website visits and 5 leads; Apartments.com and Apartment List sent 5 visits and none. These sites collect leads inside their own pages, so ask each for its lead count and any leases tied to it for October 16 to November 12. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring, and Las Vegas may run on its own rhythm, so your own months are the better guide: leads ran 54 in April and 74 in June. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhen occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many units come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits. Google Ads brought 40% of all visits and 19.4% of leads, so visits alone are a poor guide here.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level. No paid channel with spend on this page has earned that yet: Google Ads gave one lead per 274 visits against one per 92 from the listing. If the campaign cleanup brings Google Ads closer to the listing's rate, scale it in those steps.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Website Oct 16 to Nov 12 · search Oct 14 to Nov 10 · listing Oct 13 to Nov 9 (Google publishes listing figures about five days late) · unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing's number, 702.941.7223, is different from the website's 702.935.6015, so it is likely the property's call tracking number. If it is, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that call tracking report. Ask for it filtered to this number.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 919 of 1,275 organic visits (72%) and 10 of 15 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag (Oct 16 to Nov 12). See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

On the website, Google Ads sent 1,641 visits and 6 leads, one lead per 274 visits (Oct 16 to Nov 12). All 6 came from cross network campaigns; Display sent 270 visits with no leads. Against the four weeks before, visits fell 41% (2,782 to 1,641) and leads went from 8 to 6. Spend fell 9%, from $5,568 to $5,066, so each website lead cost $844 against $696 before. Performance Max sent 3 of the leads and Demand Gen 1, and 2 carry no campaign name; the two location search campaigns spent about $2,075 with none. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask for that report before judging the search campaigns. See Google Ads by campaign.

How is search trending?

Average position improved from #5.6 to #5.1. Clicks were 878 against 825 in the four weeks before (Oct 14 to Nov 10 against Sep 16 to Oct 13), as Google showed the site 4% more often and the click rate held near 10%: 9.9% against 9.7%. Clicks on searches without your name rose from 485 to 552.

Is Zillow working?

On the website, Zillow sent 213 visits and 5 leads, one lead per 43 visits (Oct 16 to Nov 12), against 248 visits and 1 lead in the four weeks before. 170 of those visits carry a Zillow tag that is filed as Unassigned. Zillow also collects leads inside Zillow, which only Zillow's own report shows. Compare both before renewing.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are each source's freshest complete read: the website through November 12, search through November 10 and the Google listing through November 9, because Google publishes listing figures about five days late. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (44, Oct 13 to Nov 9; Google publishes listing figures about five days late) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (23 visits, Oct 16 to Nov 12) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.
