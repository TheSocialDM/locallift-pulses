Where to put the ad money next

Last 4 weeks · Sep 25 to Oct 22 vs Aug 28 to Sep 24
GA4 · our read

Spend should follow cost per lease, not visits. A channel earns more budget only when a lease costs less than it is worth and its lead rate holds as spend grows.

Google Ads: move budget from campaigns with no leads to the one that convertsSpend rose 1% ($5,452 to $5,517) while website leads went from 10 to 7, so each lead cost $788 against $545 before. Demand Gen sent 3 of the 7 leads for $960, about $320 each. The In Location and Outside Location campaigns spent about $2,456 together with no website leads, and Near Me grew from $125 to $465 with none. They may still drive calls the ads account counts, so ask the Google Ads manager for that report before cutting, then shift budget toward Demand Gen and recheck cost per lead in two weeks. The Display campaign cost $104 and brought no website leads.

Fix

Other paid sources: ask Zillow, Apartments.com and Apartment List for proof before renewingZillow sent 256 website visits and 3 leads; Apartments.com and Apartment List sent 11 visits and none. These sites collect leads inside their own pages, so ask each for its lead count and any leases tied to it for September 25 to October 22. If they cannot show leases within 30 days, move part of that budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease above.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring, and Las Vegas may run on its own rhythm, so your own months are the better guide: leads ran 54 in April and 74 in June. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyWhen occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many units come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits. Google Ads brought 46% of all visits and 19.4% of leads, so visits alone are a poor guide here.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level. No paid channel here has earned that yet: Google Ads gave one lead per 399 visits against one per 151 from the listing. If the campaign cleanup brings Google Ads closer to the listing's rate, scale it in those steps.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Website Sep 25 to Oct 22 · search Sep 24 to Oct 21 · listing Sep 21 to Oct 18 · unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing's number, 702.941.7223, is different from the website's 702.935.6015, so it is likely the property's call tracking number. If it is, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that call tracking report. Ask for it filtered to this number.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 1,206 of 1,574 organic visits (77%) and 8 of 15 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag (Sep 25 to Oct 22). See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

On the website, Google Ads sent 2,790 visits and 7 leads, one lead per 399 visits (Sep 25 to Oct 22). 6 came from cross network campaigns and 1 from Search ads; Display sent 998 visits with no leads. Against the four weeks before, visits rose 3% (2,701 to 2,790) while leads went from 10 to 7. Spend rose 1%, from $5,452 to $5,517, so each website lead cost $788 against $545 before. Demand Gen sent 3 of the 7 leads; the two location search campaigns spent about $2,456 with none. The Google Ads account also counts calls from its ads, which this report does not read, so ask for that report before judging the search campaigns. See Google Ads by campaign.

How is search trending?

Average position improved from #6.6 to #5.7. Clicks were 863 against 837 in the four weeks before (Sep 24 to Oct 21 against Aug 27 to Sep 23) even as Google showed the site 16% less often, because more of the people who saw it clicked: 10.7% against 8.7%. Clicks on searches without your name rose from 459 to 507.

Is Zillow working?

On the website, Zillow sent 256 visits and 3 leads, one lead per 85 visits (Sep 25 to Oct 22). 220 of those visits carry a Zillow tag that GA4 files as Unassigned. Zillow also collects leads inside Zillow, which only Zillow's own report shows. Compare both before renewing.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are each source's freshest complete read: the website through October 22, search through October 21 and the Google listing through October 18, because Google publishes listing figures about five days late. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (46, Sep 21 to Oct 18) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (17 visits, Sep 25 to Oct 22) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.
