Search presence (verified search data)

ℹ️
What these numbers mean in plain terms: Impressions is how many times you appeared in a search result. Clicks is how many times someone actually chose you. Click through rate is the share who chose you once they saw you, so it measures how appealing your listing looks. Keywords is how many different searches you show up for at all, which is the size of your net. This week: 66 clicks from 453 impressions across the same four day window, a 14.6% click through rate at an average position of 9.8. Last week the same four days produced 43 clicks from 412 impressions at 10.4% and position 7.9. So clicks rose 54% while the average position eased about two places. That is what happens when you start ranking for more terms: the new ones enter lower down and pull the average with them, while the terms that already converted keep converting. More reach at a slightly lower average is the right trade at this stage.
