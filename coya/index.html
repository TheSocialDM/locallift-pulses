Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring. Las Vegas winters are mild, so the dip may be smaller here; your own leads by month show it as the season turns. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins. Clicks often get cheaper in winter when rivals pull back.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyIn a lease up, open homes are the reason to keep spending: hold paid search near its current level while cost per lease is acceptable. Once occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many homes come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 855.783.4811 while the website shows 702.605.6291, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. If it does, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 855.783.4811.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 541 of 787 organic visits (69%) and 2 of 11 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag (Oct 9 to Nov 5); the plain search results brought 7. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

On the website, it is the largest lead source: 10 leads from 1,208 visits, one lead per 121 visits, against 23 leads from 1,201 visits the four weeks before (Oct 9 to Nov 5 against Sep 11 to Oct 8). Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

Each source runs to its own newest complete day. Website and leads cover October 9 to November 5, search covers October 7 to November 3, and the Google listing covers October 6 to November 2 because Google publishes listing figures about five days late. The four week numbers change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (35, Google listing, Oct 6 to Nov 2) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site; Google publishes those figures about five days late. Phone taps on the website (23 visits, Oct 9 to Nov 5) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

Maps views came to 1,820 against 1,196 the four weeks before, and the week of October 27 to November 2 averaged about 82 a day, against about 70 the week before. Call button taps were 35 against 35. Last week this report said website clicks rose 21% (318 to 385, Sep 29 to Oct 26); these four weeks they came to 352 against 382. Google publishes listing figures about five days late, so these run through November 2.
