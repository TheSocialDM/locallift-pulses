Grow

Other paid sources: confirm Zillow and chinatownvegas.com before adding moneyZillow ads sent 94 visits and 4 website leads, one per 24, and also collect leads inside Zillow that this report cannot see; ask Zillow for its own lead and lease counts for September 7 to October 4. chinatownvegas.com's 7 leads came from 6 visits on two days, so some may be repeat form sends; have the leasing team confirm how many were separate renters before paying for more.

Prove it

Seasons: trim, do not stopLeasing usually slows from about November through January and picks up in spring. Las Vegas winters are mild, so the dip may be smaller here; your own leads by month will show it. Trim the weakest terms in December, keep your name and high intent searches running, and build budget in February and March ahead of spring move ins. Clicks often get cheaper in winter when rivals pull back.

Plan

Match spend to vacancyIn a lease up, open homes are the reason to keep spending: hold paid search near its current level while cost per lease is acceptable. Once occupancy is high and few leases expire in the next 60 days, cut paid search on general terms first. When many homes come open, add budget to the channel with the lowest cost per lease, not the one with the most visits.

Rule

How to scale a winnerRaise a winning channel about 15 to 20% at a time and recheck cost per lead after two weeks. If the cost climbs faster than leads, step back to the last level.

Your questions, answered

Straight answers from Google's own records
Last 4 weeks · Sep 7 to Oct 4 unless an answer names a month
What happens after a renter taps Call on the listing?

Google counts taps on the Call button; it cannot see whether a call connected or booked a tour. The listing shows 855.783.4811 while the website shows 702.605.6291, so the listing most likely carries its own call tracking number. If it does, answered calls, missed calls and recordings are in that number's call report. Ask for it filtered to 855.783.4811.

Isn't "Organic Search" our website SEO?

Only partly. 635 of 952 organic visits (67%) and 3 of 4 organic leads started on the Google listing, which links to the site with its own tag. See the Credit check.

Is Google Ads working?

On the website, yes: 23 leads from 1,187 visits, one lead per 52 visits, against 11 leads from 3,361 visits the four weeks before. It is the largest lead source. Spend was not shared for either window, so the cost per lead now vs before is not known yet; enter it in Cost per lead, or ask the ads vendor to link Google Ads to Google Analytics so it appears here every Friday.

Why do some cards show four weeks and others whole months?

The four week numbers are the freshest complete read and change every Friday. The monthly charts show the longer trend and add a month once it closes. Each card's date line says which one you are looking at.

Are phone taps on the website the same as Call button taps?

No. Call button taps (34) happen on the Google listing before a renter reaches the site. Phone taps on the website (22 visits) happen after they arrive. One renter can do both, so the two are reported apart and never added together.

How is the Google listing trending?

Renters are acting on it more: website clicks from the listing rose 13% (335 to 377) and Call button taps rose from 29 to 34. Views in search held near level, while Maps views eased from about 76 a day in August to about 40 a day from August 31 on. Google's data does not show a cause. Next: LocalLift reviews the listing's Maps categories, map pin, photos and hours, and checks Maps views per day on October 16.

What is chinatownvegas.com?

A neighborhood site that sent 69 visits and 7 leads. All 7 leads came on September 14 and 17 from 6 visits, so some may be the same person sending the form more than once. The leasing team can confirm how many were separate renters.
