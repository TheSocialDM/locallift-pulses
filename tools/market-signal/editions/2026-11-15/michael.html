THE MARKET SIGNAL  ·  NO. 04Being seen vs. being chosen

Your price changed. Did the internet notice?

1.4%Down in two months. The Phoenix area rent index fell 1.4% from August to October 2026, per the Bureau of Labor Statistics.¹ It tracks rents paid, existing leases included.
When rents move, the new price has to travel. A renter may still find the old number on a listing, a Google profile or an AI answer. Three checks this week:
01

Ask the AIAsk an AI assistant what a one bedroom at your community costs. Does the answer match today's price?

02

Search like a renterSearch your community name on a phone and open the first three results. Do they show the same price and the same special?

03

Date the offerIf there is a current special, does your Google profile show it as an offer with its end date?

1. U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI, Phoenix area, October 2026, Nov 10, 2026. One reading, not seasonally adjusted.
