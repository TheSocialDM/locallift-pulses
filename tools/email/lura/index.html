The count matters less than the rhythm, and this is why. Google weighs how recently reviews arrived as well as how many there are, so a stalled flow works against you rather than simply pausing. Direction requests are the strongest free signal a listing produces, because someone asking for directions intends to turn up. Website clicks from the listing eased 18% to 165, and that is a second straight fall after 235 and then 201. Profile views were level at 2,360 against 2,396, so the change sits on the listing itself rather than on how often Google put it in front of anyone.

Your search numbers are refreshed and they moved in two directions at once. Across the 28 days to September 16 your average position improved from 10.4 to 6.9 and your click through rate rose from 7.63% to 9.15%. In plain terms, when Google shows you now, renters pick you roughly one time in eleven, which is better than it has been. Over the same 28 days you were shown to 36% fewer people and your clicks fell from 1,182 to 911. Ranking higher on a smaller footprint is not a bad week on its own, but clicks are what fill homes, so that is the line we are watching rather than explaining away.
