The short version: the offer gap we have been writing about for three weeks has closed, and I am retiring that recommendation. Your concession across the window is 4.6% against a 4.4% market, where three weeks ago you stood 4.2 points above it. Both sides moved: yours came down from 12.4% and the market's came down from 8.2%. Comparing what is advertised today rather than the window average tells the same story, ten of the twenty communities we track are promoting 8 to 12 weeks free right now, and five of them are deeper than your 8 weeks. There is no longer a cut to make here, and making one would cost you rent.

The good news is what that has done to your net rent. You ask $2.36 a square foot against a $2.18 market and you now keep $2.26 of it against $2.09, which is 8.1% ahead and 4th of the twenty-one communities in the report. Three weeks ago the same asking premium was arriving as 1.5% because the offer was consuming it. Please read the raw dollar comparison with care as always: your homes average 709 square feet against 770 across the set, so the $3 difference between your $1,605 blended net effective rent and the $1,608 set average is floor plan, not pricing, in either direction.

What has not closed is the two-bedroom line, and it is now the whole of the story. Sixteen homes, 75.0% leased, 31.3% of them available right now, sitting at 220.7 days on market against a 104.6 day market. Palmcroft is the sharpest version of it: all three of its homes are available, none of its residents renewed, and it has been on the market 180 days. Those two-bedrooms ask $2.28 a square foot against a corridor two-bedroom average of $2.06, which is 10.7% above it, and that is the widest gap of your three unit types. Closing the days-on-market gap is worth about $83,000 a year, applied only to the 9 two-bedroom homes that actually turn in a year at their 43.7% renewal rate rather than to all sixteen. That figure is measured on the advertised-listing basis; on the in-place rent roll the same homes read 172 days and the number is nearer $48,000. Treat it as a band.

Whatever you do there, please keep it away from the one-bedrooms. Willo is fully leased with 3.2% available and leases in 45 days. Coronado is 98.8% leased and leases in 55. That is 182 of your 227 homes, 80% of the building, leasing at or inside the market's pace while asking 4.5% above the corridor per foot. A blanket offer spends the most money exactly where absorption is strongest. Half this corridor already gates by home, term or move-in date: one nearby community advertises ten weeks free and realises nothing at all, another advertises twelve and realises 2.1%. Advertise the number and restrict who gets it.

Reviews are the one thing on this report that needs you, and they need you every week until the number moves. Your rating is 4.7 stars, re-read this week, and it is the highest of all twenty-one communities in the report against a 3.4 average. That rating is an asset most of your competitors do not have, and it is the argument for discounting less rather than more. I want to be straight with you about one thing: I did not re-read your review count against the live listing this week, so I am not going to put a number or a target in front of you that I have not checked. That returns next week with the count, the pace over the last six months and the nearest fast mover by name. The two asks do not depend on it and they should become habit now: one at every renewal conversation, one every time a work order closes. Nothing else on this page is blocked on you. This is.

Two notes. First, all seven of your floor plans still carry a negative trade-out, unchanged at -3.72% across the book. Your offer moved over this window and that number did not, which is a reason not to attribute it to the offer in either direction, but it is the one I would watch: occupancy is the lagging number and it looks fine, trade-out is the leading one and it is still pointing down. Second, your studios are the quieter version of the two-bedroom problem, 29 homes at 118 days against an 84 day market, asking 8.5% above the corridor. Worth watching before acting.
